## The problem

Customers who need tailoring, custom clothing, fabric, accessories or training may have difficulty finding suitable providers and comparing what they offer. Providers also need practical ways to display their work and respond to enquiries.

## The application

9jaTailors is a marketplace project connecting customers with tailoring and fashion-related vendors. The application architecture distinguishes customer, vendor and administrator experiences.

Project components include:

- Listings and vendor profiles for tailors, designers and related suppliers
- Listings for clothing designs, fabrics, sewing machines, accessories, training and opportunities
- Search and discovery interfaces
- Account registration and sign-in
- Vendor listing and approval-related workflows
- Customer enquiries and contact-oriented interactions
- Responsive layouts for browsing on desktop and mobile
